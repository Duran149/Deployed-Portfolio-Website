ABOUT THE OFFICE

Maramag Information and
Communication Technology Office

The Municipal Information and Communications Technology Office supports the Municipality of Maramag through technology, digital services, computer networking, technical support, and information systems development.

Web Application

This web application is a digital portfolio and information platform created to showcase information about the Municipal Information and Communications Technology Office. It provides an organized presentation of the office's purpose, services, vision, mission, technical activities, and technology-related development.

💻

Purpose

The purpose of the Municipal Information and Communications Technology Office is to provide reliable technical assistance, maintain computer systems and networks, support digital government services, and help improve the use of information technology within the municipality.

The office also promotes efficient, secure, and accessible technology services for municipal employees, offices, residents, and other stakeholders.

🌐

Vision

A digitally empowered Municipality of Maramag where information and communication technology contributes to efficient public service, innovation, connectivity, and sustainable community development.

🎯

Mission

To provide dependable information and communication technology services, technical support, digital solutions, computer networking, and information systems that improve government operations and public service delivery.

ICT Services and Activities

Computer Technical Support

Installation, maintenance, troubleshooting, and technical support for computers and other IT equipment.

Network Installation

Installation and maintenance of network cables, switches, routers, and other networking equipment.

Digital Services

Development and support of digital systems and technology-based services for municipal operations.

Web Development

Creation and maintenance of websites and web-based applications for information and public services.

Information Systems

Development and support of information systems that help organize, manage, and access government data.

Technology Development

Implementation of innovative technology solutions to improve productivity and government services.
