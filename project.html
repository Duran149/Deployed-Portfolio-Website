01 FACULTY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

STUDENT'S GRADING SYSTEM: A Digital Grade Management Platform for K-12 Faculty Members

System Developer / Database Desginer / Web and Mobile Application Developer

Problem Statement

Teachers and Faculty Members are responsible for recording, computing, monitoring, and managing student grades throughout the academic year. Traditional manual grading methods using paper records and spreadsheets may require significant time and effort and can increase the possibility of computational errors or incorrect grade records.


K-12 faculty members need an organized and reliable system that can assist them in managing student information, recording assessment scores, automatically computing grades, and generating accounts grading reports based on the required grading components.


The Student's Grading System is proposed as a digital grade management platform exclusively designed for authorized K-12 faculty memebers. The system also to provide an efficient, organized, and secure way to record, calculate, monitor, and manage student academic performance.

Technologies & Tools Used

Web Application Mobile Application HTML CSS JavaScript PHP MySQL Database Management Cloud Database User Authentication Automated Grade Computation Data Management Report Generation Responsive Design Faculty Dashbaoard

Key Contributions

  • Designed a digital grading management system exclusively intended for suthorized K-12 faculty members.
  • Developed a secure faculty login and authentication system to protect student academic records.
  • Designed a student information management system for organizing student profiles, sections, and subjedt.
  • Developed automated gradee computation to reduce manual calculation and minimize computational errors.
  • Integrated grading components for recording quizzes, activities, performance tasks, examinations, and other academic assessments.
  • Designed a faculty dashboard for monitoring student grade and economic performances.
  • Developed student performance monitoring to identity learners who may need additional academic support.
  • Created grade reports that can be reviewed and generated by authorized faculty members.
  • Implemented secure database storage for student records and academic grading information.

Outcome & Impact

The expected outcome of the project is a functional digital Student's Grading System that assists K-12 faculty members in recording, computing, organizing, and monitoring students academic performance.


The system aims to reduce the time required for annual grade computation while improving the organization and accuracy of academic records. Faculty members can manage grading information through a centralized and user-friendly digital platform.


By providing automated computation, organized student records, performance monitoring, and report generation, the project aims to support teachers and faculty members in efficiently managing student academic information while maintaining secure access to sensitive educational records.

Main System Features
  • Faculty-Only Access: The system is reserved for authorized K-12 faculty members and administrators through secure user authentication.
  • Student Information Management: Allows faculty members to organize student profiles, sections, subjects, and academic information.
  • Automated Grade Computation: Automatically calculates student grades based on recorded assessment scores and grading components.
  • Assessment Recording: Allows teachers to record quizzes, activities, performances, tasks, examinations, and other academic requirements.
  • Student Performance Monitoring: Helps faculty memebers monitor student academic progress and overall performances.
  • Grade Reports: Generates organized academic grade reports for authorized faculty members.
  • Secure Databse: Stores student and grading information securely within a centralized databse system.
Evidence / Expected Sample Output

The project will demonstrate a functional digital grading platform featuring faculty authentication, student information management, subject and section organization, assessment score recording, automated grade computation, academic performance monitoring, faculty dashboards, grade reports, and secure database management designed specifically for K-12 educational institutions.

03 CAPSTONE PROJECT

HOME ALONE: An IoT-Based Home Safety, Health Monitoring, and Emergency Response System for Children and Elderly Persons

Capstone Project Developer / IoT System Designer

Problem Statement

Single parents, working parents, caregivers, and busy individuals may sometimes need to leave a child or elderly person temporarily alone at home while attending work, buying groceries, running errands, or handling other important responsibilities. Traditional security cameras mainly provide surveillance and often require continuous human monitoring. They may not automatically detect dangerous situations such as fire, smoke, gas leakage, unauthorized door activity, entry into dangerous areas, or health-related emergencies.


Home Alone is proposed as an IoT-based smart home safety, health monitoring, and emergency response system that can detect important events and automatically notify parents, caregivers, and registered emergency contacts.

Technologies & Tools Used

ESP32 IoT Artificial Intelligence Computer Vision AI Camera Gas Sensor Smoke Sensor Temperature Sensor Door Sensor Smart Bracelet Mobile Application Web Dashboard Cloud Database Real-Time Notifications GPS / Location Service

Key Contributions

  • Designed an IoT-based home monitoring and emergency response system.
  • Proposed AI camera monitoring for detecting the presence and movement of children or elderly persons.
  • Developed configurable danger zones such as kitchens, staircases, balconies, and other hazardous areas.
  • Integrated fire, smoke, gas leakage, temperature, and door monitoring sensors.
  • Designed a smart emergency bracelet with health monitoring and an SOS emergency button.
  • Proposed two-way communication between the parent and the monitored person through a microphone and speaker.
  • Designed an emergency dashboard displaying emergency status, sensor information, home location, and camera images.
  • Implemented emergency notifications for parents, caregivers, and registered emergency contacts.
  • Proposed smart door monitoring and an emergency door-release mechanism to support safe evacuation.

Outcome & Impact

The expected outcome of the project is a functional prototype of an IoT-based smart home safety and emergency monitoring system. The system will provide parents and caregivers with real-time information about household hazards and the safety of a child or elderly person who is temporarily left at home.


Unlike conventional security cameras that mainly allow users to observe their homes, Home Alone is designed to detect important events, determine their severity, send emergency notifications, and support faster communication and response.


The project aims to provide an additional layer of protection for children and elderly persons while giving parents and caregivers greater awareness and a faster way to respond during emergencies.

Main System Features
  • AI Camera Monitoring: Detects the monitored person's presence, movement, and entry into predefined danger zones.
  • Fire and Smoke Detection: Detects possible fire or smoke and activates emergency alerts.
  • Gas Leak Detection: Detects potentially dangerous gas levels and immediately notifies the parent or caregiver.
  • Smart Emergency Bracelet: Provides health monitoring, an SOS emergency button, microphone, and speaker.
  • Two-Way Communication: Allows communication between the parent and the monitored person during emergencies.
  • Smart Door Lock and Sensor: Monitors unexpected door activity and can support emergency door release for evacuation.
  • Emergency Dashboard: Displays emergency status, sensor readings, home location, camera images, and emergency contact information.
  • Emergency Notifications: Sends alerts to parents, caregivers, and registered nearby emergency contacts.
Evidence / Expected Sample Output

The project will demonstrate an IoT-based monitoring system with sensor data, AI camera monitoring, smart bracelet functionality, emergency alerts, a mobile or web dashboard, emergency event history, registered home location, and prototype emergency response features.
